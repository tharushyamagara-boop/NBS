SUNCASA Kigali NbS Impact Platform

SUNCASA (Scaling Urban Nature-Based Solutions for Climate Adaptation in Sub-Saharan Africa) is funded by Global Affairs Canada and jointly led by IISD and WRI, implemented in Kigali alongside the City of Kigali and the Rwanda Forestry Authority.

All public monitoring data released under Open Data principles for the Government of Rwanda and climate adaptation partners.
