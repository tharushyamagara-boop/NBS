It is with great enthusiasm that I submit this technical and financial proposal in response to the Request for Proposals for the **Development of a Digital Dashboard to Communicate the Impact of Nature-Based Solutions in Kigali** under the **SUNCASA** project, funded by **Global Affairs Canada** and jointly led by the **International Institute for Sustainable Development (IISD)** and the **World Resources Institute (WRI)**, in partnership with the **City of Kigali** and the **Rwanda Forestry Authority (RFA)**.

My work bridges complex ecological data, spatial analytics, and non-expert public storytelling to ensure that decision-makers, community leaders, and funding partners can immediately grasp and act upon environmental investments.

### Why This Bid Offers Unrivaled Value & Certainty

1. **Working Prototype Already Built & Validated:**  
   Unlike traditional bids offering conceptual wireframes, our team has already developed a **fully functional, interactive Minimum Viable Product (MVP) dashboard** adhering precisely to your Terms of Reference. The prototype features:
   - Complete indicators across the four core themes: **Climate Adaptation**, **Biodiversity Protection**, **Gender Equality & Social Inclusion (GESI)**, and **Employment & Economic Opportunities**.
   - Spatial GIS mapping of Kigali's critical micro-catchments in the **Lower Nyabarongo River watershed** (Yanze, Mpazi, Mount Kigali, Nyabugogo, and the 30-meter riparian corridor) using Leaflet.
   - Deep alignment with the **MyPeg (www.mypeg.ca)** indicator-driven, narrative-supported storytelling methodology (answering: *What is this indicator? Why does it matter for Kigali? What is SUNCASA doing?*).
   - Seamless, instant bilingual switching between **English** and **Ikinyarwanda** with zero page reload latency.
   - Built-in data export (Indicators JSON and Catchment GeoJSON) and a tailored print stylesheet for 1-click executive briefs.
   This drastically derisks project delivery and guarantees that our inception phase starts with tangible, testable assets rather than abstract discussions.

2. **Native RFA FMES Interoperability & Zero Vendor Lock-In:**  
   The proposed solution utilizes an open, modular client-side architecture (HTML5, Vanilla CSS3, modern ES6+ JavaScript, Leaflet, and Chart.js). It avoids proprietary licenses, expensive recurring cloud fees, or heavy server infrastructures. Forestry compartment registries (e.g., `COMP-GAS-JB-01`), silvicultural types, and audited survival indicators directly mirror the data dictionary of the RFA's **Forest Management and Evaluation System (FMES)**, ensuring seamless integration with ongoing FMES upgrade phases.

3. **Strict Fiscal Alignment with the RFP Envelope:**  
   Our financial proposal of **USD 19,850** (inclusive of all applicable taxes) fits precisely within the **USD 20,000 maximum budget ceiling**. The pricing provides transparent Level of Effort (LOE) daily rates, clear deliverable milestones, and a distinguished breakdown between core MVP build activities (USD 18,350) and optional hosting/maintenance transition support (USD 1,500).

4. **Deep Commitment to GESI & Local Ownership:**  
   Our delivery methodology treats GESI not as an afterthought, but as a central narrative pillar—highlighting female nursery leadership, equitable green labor wages, and youth GIS stewardship. Furthermore, our handover process includes dedicated administrative training and bilingual documentation so RFA and City of Kigali personnel can effortlessly update data and narrative text independently.

We confirm that this proposal remains valid for 90 days from the submission deadline of September 16, 2026. We are ready to commence work immediately upon contract award and look forward to collaborating closely with IISD, WRI, RFA, and City of Kigali stakeholders to deliver a world-class communication platform.
